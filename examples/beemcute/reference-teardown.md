## Look

- Near-black ground lit by slow-drifting radial gradients (peach → coral → magenta → purple), soft vignette, fine film grain.
- One neo-grotesk sans throughout, tight tracking; statements centred, short, often a white first half and a gradient second half.
- Product UI is real but staged: pieces float at different depths with depth-of-field blur, and the camera finds one focal element at a time.
- Glows mark importance: the Publish button, the generated image, the highlighted leaderboard row.

## Motion

- Nothing is ever still: holds keep a slow push or drift.
- Text enters word by word out of blur, or typed with a caret; giant single words focus-pull from heavy blur.
- Camera: push-ins on UI to make small text huge, pull-backs to reveal context, depth dollies through floating cards; motion blur on fast moves.
- Transitions: hard cuts on beats, blur dissolves, one full-frame light wash before the finale.

## Pacing and sound (measured)

- 28 shots in 56 s: mean 2.05 s, range 0.4–6.2 s. Five acts, each with one idea.
- Music ≈ 100 BPM (beat 0.6 s, bar 2.4 s), one track throughout.
- Several cuts land on bar lines (2.57, 7.37, 26.5, 55.4 s). Timbre section changes at 4.96, 7.36, 31.36, 52.97 s line up with the story's turns ("Just an idea?" at 7.37; Reach at ~31; the summary at ~53).
- The music drops out briefly at 4.6 s and 9.7 s, just before new lines land.
- Individual shot cuts are not measurably beat-locked in this recording (median 152 ms from a beat, chance level); screen recordings can shift audio against video, so trust the section-level sync, not frame-level.
- Effects visible in the spectrogram: risers at ~26–31 s and ~45–50 s building into the next section, bright shimmer hits at ~19, 40, 42.5 and 47 s.

## Take, translate, leave

- **Take:** the five-act arc (reframe → hook → show the product working → what it unlocks → summary + logo), staged floating UI, kinetic type, the push-in/pull-back grammar, glow on the one important thing, music-led turns.
- **Translate:** warm peach/magenta → Beemcute night and blue; the reference's grid lines → our contour lines; "Build, Publish, Earn" → "Every request. One platform. Complete visibility."
- **Leave:** its copy, game imagery, logo, wordmark animation, and exact shot order.
